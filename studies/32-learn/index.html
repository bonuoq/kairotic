The descriptor distance is how far a patch sounds from the target (standardised descriptors, per number, as in Study 30); the patch distance is the mean difference of its parameters in [0, 1]. The target itself, rendered with another noise, is the floor: no patch can sound closer than that. The network is trained to guess the numbers, so it can land between two patches that sound alike; the search then listens, rendering the real synth, and moves towards what sounds closer. Nothing in it is specific to this synth: the same search would refine a faust~ patch (Study 31) found by any first guess.
