    About

    Study 55. The second study of discussion #217: the continuum of Study 52 made deterministic and coherent between the tree and MEI. A line becomes a tree of kairotic.mt (the app's), written on a grid in measures: a note is one per measure it sounds in, tied into the next, a free note keeps its exact Hz (its name is chosen when it is written, by kairotic.spell), and its first piece keeps where it really sounded (:tstamp.real, :dur.real, named after MEI's attributes), its loudness (:vel) and its glissando. The voice carries the line's resolutions, wires and electronics. Its MEI is the one the app writes (mt/->mei: measures, spelling, ties, beams) with MEI's own gestural attributes where MEI has them (@tstamp.real, @dur.real, @vel, a mirror other software can read) and one <extMeta type="kairotic"> holding, as EDN, what MEI has no place for, keyed by the xml:id of each note. Reading takes the written music from the notation and the rest from extMeta; a MEI written elsewhere reads as its notation. So tree → MEI → tree gives the tree back and MEI → tree → MEI the MEI, byte for byte, and the line comes back exactly. The staff of the drawing takes the names the MEI gives, so drawing and engraving agree.

    What it leaves open

    • Reading a MEI from elsewhere covers notes, rests, chords, tuplets, beams, ties, meters and keys; dynamics, directions, slurs and lyrics are not read yet.
    • Several staves and layers read as voices; the continuum is still one line.
    • Where <extMeta> sits is a choice to check against the MEI schema; <extData> beside each note is the other one.
