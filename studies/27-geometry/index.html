Tonnetz triads as triangles, the path of the harmony

Click a pitch class on the clock or a node of the Tonnetz to hear it. Drag the slider to move through the work without playing. The example walks a hexatonic cycle (C, c, A♭, a♭, E, e) by single-note moves P and L, then a diatonic cadence: on the Tonnetz the first half turns around one spot and the second half travels. New shapes tried here, beyond the current vocabulary: :opacity on any shape, :curve (a smooth path through points), [:group {:rotate :translate :scale} shapes] and :hit, which names a shape the component receives when it is pressed.
