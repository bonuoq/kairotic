talking in a room: text, push-to-talk, voice notes, video — discussion #135 loading…

What is said in a room is ephemeral: it lives in the pages that are there. Something stays only when someone keeps it — a message as an annotation (anchored where it points: @b3 a box, #12 a point of the history, ^33 a beat), a voice note as a sound usable as a sample, a video frame as an image. What is yours is kept at once; keeping what is someone else's asks them first. The pages talk directly (WebRTC); the relay only introduces them.
