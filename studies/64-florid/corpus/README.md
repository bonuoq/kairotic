# Fux's florid examples

The twelve two-part examples of fifth species (florid counterpoint) in Johann Joseph Fux's *Gradus ad Parnassum*
(Vienna, 1725), Mann's figures 82–88, in Humdrum `**kern`: `fux/gap_*.krn`, copied unchanged from Mark Gotham's
encoding of the *Gradus* — [MarkGotham/species](https://github.com/MarkGotham/species) (commit `5c7cae4`, `I/sp5`),
released by Mark Gotham and FourScoreAndMore.org into the public domain (CC0 1.0), as each file's header says.

`a` the cantus firmus in the lower part, `b` in the upper; the study reads the six with it below (figure 87's both
have it above: its cantus firmus is still one of the study's), each in its mode, with its musica ficta.

`node studies/64-florid/corpus/fit.mjs` measures them (`kairotic/measure.mjs`) and fits the engine's costs to them:
`FUX_COSTS` in `measure.mjs`.
