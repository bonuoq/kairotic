Compile the voice and realise the score.

Each lane is what features~ and pitch~ hear, frame by frame (Meyda on 2048 samples every 1024; YIN): loudness is the level of a frame, brightness its spectral centroid, noisiness its spectral flatness (0 a tone, 1 white noise). So a recording can be read into the same lanes and written again by another voice. The realiser plays a bank of steady settings of the voice and listens to each; for every frame it blends the settings nearest to the pitch, brightness and noisiness written (where a pitch is written, the ones in which YIN would hear it), makes the level up with gain, and plays it; then it listens to what it played and asks, frame by frame, for more where it fell short (three passes, the best kept). Held still is the same voice at the mean of what is written: what following the curves is worth. Where a lane is shaded the voice has no setting that sounds like that — noise as dark as a tone, say: brightness and noisiness are not independent in it.
