About discussion #256

  
An agent box receives a melody and a request, and Claude builds, inside it, the machine that answers: a sub-patch of boxes you can open, read and run without Claude. The boxes it builds with carry the rules of the earlier studies: bass is Study 51's two-part counterpoint turned upside down (the melody given, the bass written under it, its futures counted, the engine choosing by the soft rules of Studies 38–42 and three of style: the degrees ① ④ ⑤, the melody a chord tone, the harmonic rhythm); inner realizes the figures as chords in four parts by the rule of the octave, with the cadential 6/4, led by the shortest way without parallels. Where none of them does what is asked (a texture, an Alberti bass), Claude writes a code box or a class of its own in :defs.

  
    
    
  • Claude builds the machine, not the harmonization: once built, the patch runs again with no call, deterministically (a seed), and the recipe — the request, the melody — is kept beside it.
