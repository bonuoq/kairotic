      About this study
      
Three levels of graphics. Inside a box, a component describes its view as shapes (kairotic.shapes), here with one new shape, :heat, for lists of numbers. Above or under the patch, a layer holds marks anchored to boxes and projections of a box's view at another size. Behind everything, the patch may have one background shader, the only WebGL on the page.

      
Every edit is an event of the history; the knob's value is a parameter and stays out of it.
