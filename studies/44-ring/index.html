    About
    
  
    Study 44. A ring keeps the messages that reach it with their time, from the first one (or the first after :clear), and forgets what is older than what it keeps: circular in time, not in a count of messages (the last n messages are xf/partition n 1). Write with the keys or the value, and read it three ways:
    
  
    pipe sends each message d after it came in, with the d it had then: a shorter d lets a later message overtake an earlier one. tape moves a read head d behind the write head and sends what it crosses, in the order it meets it: drag the delay slowly and the music is compressed or stretched; drag it back faster than time goes and the head goes backwards, the notes again, reversed; a jump of d (typed, or a click on the slider) is a seek, which sends nothing of what lies between, as the patterns player does. tap sends nothing by itself: a bang (the button, or a clock) asks what was there d ago — held, or, between numbers, the value between the two around it, which follows the glide of the value.
    
  
    What it decides
    
  
      
    
    • Which reading the ring box has by default, and whether the others are arguments (ring 8 tape) or other boxes.
