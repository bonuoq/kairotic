About

The phrases are the ones the tables were not counted on (every fifth chorale). The bass is Study 63's under.mjs: an exact bridge under the soprano, each quarter a local key, a bass note and a chord over it, its costs how often Bach does each thing in the other chorales (@kairotic/rules' continuo.mjs) — the chord over each degree of the bass, the soprano over the bass, their moves together, the cadence —, the tonal functions read in the local keys; the soprano a chord tone, or a suspension, a passing note or a note held over a moving bass, as his are. The figures are written as Bach abbreviates them in his continuo parts (the Bach Chorales Figured Bass dataset): nothing over a 5/3, 6 for a 6/3, 6/4/2 for a 4/2, an accidental alone for the 3rd; under Bach's own phrase, his figures where his continuo part survives (BCFB), else read from his four voices. The inner voices are bach-4's, the bass's chords asked as a wish. The variants turn a layer of the bass off: no-functions, no-keys (every quarter in the chorale's key), no-economy. The phrases are from Craig Sapp's edition (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), Bach's figures from the BCFB dataset (CC BY 4.0).
