MIDI in and out as Kairotic events — the first step of discussion #135 loading…

Everything that comes in or goes out is one EDN map in the shape of a pattern event: pitch as :freq in Hz, loudness as :amp, a voice as :id. A bend, MPE or a just interval is only a different :freq. Without MIDI hardware, turn on loopback: what goes out as bytes is read back in, so the round trip is heard and drawn.
