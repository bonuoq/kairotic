    About
    
  
    The history is what happened: the notes chosen, by you or by the engine, with their branches. The futuribles are what could happen from each point of it: they are never stored, they are a pure function of a point of the history and a rule set, and the only bridge between the two is a choice, which becomes a point of the history. This page writes a canon: the dux is the line being written, the comes is the dux a few notes later, shifted by an interval, so every rule between the voices falls on the dux compared with itself. Rules are data (first species, after Fux): the hard ones make a note legal or not, with a reason; the soft ones give it a cost. Leaps are not penalised for being leaps, only compensated by contrary motion (a 4th or a 5th turns back, better by step); what costs is a line that stays still, hovers, runs one way or repeats itself mechanically, and the memory of the work makes a figure it used earlier cheaper. The engine weighs each note with the cheapest way the line can go on after it (looks ahead), so that a cheap note leading into a corridor of repeated notes is avoided. Invertible counterpoint is a transform of the voices checked by the same rules: at the 10th parallel 3rds become octaves, at the 12th a 6th becomes a 7th. The state owes things: a leap is recovered by step, the dux ends on the final. Hard rules look only at the last notes, so the ways to finish from any point are counted exactly; their log₂ is the divergence, the bits of freedom left, shown under the score. The engine chooses among the futuribles that still have a future, with a seed and a temperature, which can follow the divergence as in Copycat: colder where the work is tight. Modes on the white keys, without musica ficta. The work is kept in this browser.
