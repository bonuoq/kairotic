Live, the sound is not kept: what a work can keep of a take is the stream of messages to shift~, each at its time, the same messages a patch would send through its inlet (factors, transpose, progression, formants, dry). Factors are ratios (3/2), semitones with a sign (+7, 0 for the unison) or cents (386c); a progression is one line per step, seconds: factors, looped. The node works one frame late (shift~ above); the browser and the sound card add their own buffers (system, as the browser reports them), and a Bluetooth headset adds 150–300 ms more that the browser often does not report. Measure latency plays clicks through the speakers and hears them in the microphone: the real round trip of this computer, plus shift~, is what you hear (heard). For the least: wired headphones or the built-in output, frame 512, and an audio interface rather than the built-in microphone; the analysis is the same as in Study 28 and gives the same samples as the offline shift.
