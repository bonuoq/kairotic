About discussion #341

A Schrödinger bridge is the most likely random process from a given start to a given end, relative to a reference process that says which moves are natural. On a layered graph — a state a unit — it is the product of two messages, one forwards from the start, one backwards from the end. The futuribles (Study 38) already count the ways to the end exactly: that is the backward message with every way weighing one. Here each way weighs exp(−cost / temperature), the cost being the soft rules of every engine (soft.mjs, through the kernel's own costs); the hard rules weigh nothing (refused), so whatever a bridge draws is legal, and it never gets stuck: every note it offers has a future.

  • Varying from a level (Study 61) is a bridge through waypoints: the notes that survive are pinned, each span between them is drawn with its true probability — no backtracking.
  • A morph keeps the beginning of one phrase and the end of another, and draws the way between them under the rules of every phrase.
  • The economy (#342, #399): a bar's figure — its shape from its first note — is a word, and the forward draw prefers a figure the line has already said (bits.mjs, the measure Studies 61 and 64 share), so the way between two phrases is said with fewer figures, a sequence where it can: a morph from the C major incipit to the 7–6 chain goes from 62 bits in 8 different figures without it to 47 in 5 with it (20 draws). The facts say each line's figures in bits.
  • Above the economy, the tonal functions (functions.mjs, @kairotic/rules): each unit's chord as its bass and the melody's note imply it, and a succession the grammar of a tonal phrase refuses (a dominant taken back by a subdominant, an applied dominant unresolved) costs more — a unit's cost, so the bridge stays exact. A morph from the C major incipit to the 7–6 chain broke the grammar about once a line with the economy alone; with the functions, almost never.
  • Smooth: a leap beyond a 3rd costs more (a unit's cost, exact), so a morph or a variation goes by steps where it can, into the notes it keeps too.
  • Between two rhythms the states are rhythms and a step is a swap: with the metre weighed, the way passes through rhythms that sit well in the bar; without it, any way of the right length.
  • What comes next (#341): the bridge as the engine of florid counterpoint (#342), presets, spectra and kairoi (continuous bridges), and the tiles of an endless zoom.
