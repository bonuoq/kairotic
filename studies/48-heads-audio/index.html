About

Study 48. Study 47's model with sound: a span of samples — a ring~ (the last seconds) or a fixed work — and heads, each a head of its own. A writer writes what it listens to (the input, or what a reader sends times its feed) on every sample it passes, as a tape head: silence too, so a writer that hears nothing erases. It replaces what is there, or with mix keeps it, times keep. A reader sounds where it is; following a writer, d behind, then its speed. Here speed is pitch as well as time: 0.5 is an augmentation an octave down, −1 is backwards. A new d glides (the pitch bends) or, larger than the jump, seeks with a crossfade. Two writers from both ends of a fixed span write a palindrome in sound: its second half is the first one reversed. A writer listening to its reader is feedback: a delay's echoes, and with feed 1 a round that never ends.

What it decides

  • A writer is a tape head: it writes every sample it passes, silence too (an ovr writer that hears nothing erases).
  • mix x: what was there, times x, plus what comes in — 1 an overdub, less a sound that fades at each pass.
  • Readers read before writers write, so a reader d behind meets the sound exactly d later and feedback is exact.
