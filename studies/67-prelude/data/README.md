# The prelude of Study 67

`bwv846-prelude.json` is Study 66's (`../../66-compression/data/`): J. S. Bach, *Das Wohltemperierte Clavier I*,
Prelude 1 in C, BWV 846, as notes `[[onset duration midi] …]` in 16ths, read from the **Mutopia Project**'s
public-domain typeset (`ftp/BachJS/BWV846/wtk1-prelude1`: Shay Rojansky, Han-Wen Nienhuys, Tobias Erbsland) by Study
66's `tools/ly2notes.py`. A copy, so that the study stands on its own.
