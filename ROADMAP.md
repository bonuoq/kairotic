# Roadmap

*[Leer en castellano](ROADMAP.es.md)*

What comes after **0.3.0-alpha** (December 2026). This is a direction, not a schedule: each line lives in
an open discussion, where its design is decided and where you can join in. New ideas start in
[Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/categories/ideas); as usual, an idea is
tried first as a study and moves into a package or the core once it has proved itself.

## Next

- **Following a performer.** Following by features and a rehearsal mode, where the score learns how it
  sounds ([#308](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/308)); after score following
  ([#204](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/204)) and takes
  ([#289](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/289)).
- **Form, open and conducted.** A layer of form — states, transitions, instructions, regions
  ([#284](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/284)), after the open-form works of Study 70
  ([#212](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/212)) — towards conducted improvisation and
  Soundpainting over the network ([#213](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/213)).
- **Live coding.** A text-only session that reveals the patch and its history when the set ends
  ([#407](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/407)); messages seen on the code and the patch
  ([#183](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/183)).

## Ongoing

- **Composition engines.** One constraint engine for every style
  ([#353](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/353)), from partimento to chorales and chorale
  preludes ([#301](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/301),
  [#342](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/342)); analysis and reduction as the other side of
  composing ([#321](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/321),
  [#354](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/354),
  [#399](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/399)); bridges between materials
  ([#341](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/341)).
- **Notation.** Lenses: several notations of one tree
  ([#380](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/380)); hybrid notation and drawn synchronisation
  with live electronics ([#217](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/217)).
- **History and data.** Datomic's ideas for the database, the room transactor among them
  ([#382](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/382)); compacting large histories
  ([#298](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/298)); lines as sub-patches
  ([#117](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/117)); a work as a file
  ([#149](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/149)).
- **Claude in Kairotic.** One Claude for every box ([#351](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/351)),
  the agent box ([#256](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/256)) and incipit, a history grown from
  a given material to choose from ([#296](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/296)). Always with
  each person's own key or account.
- **The repository.** Organizing `studies/`, and when a study becomes its own app
  ([#416](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/416)).

Everything else under discussion is in [Ideas](https://github.com/bonuoq/kairotic/discussions/categories/ideas);
what has been done, in the [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
